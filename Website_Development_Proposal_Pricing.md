## Project Assumptions

The above timelines and prices are based on the following:

- Client provides finalized **content, images, product information and design/mockup** where applicable.
- Development begins after all required materials and access are provided.
- Prices cover the features specifically listed in the selected plan.
- Shopify subscription, WordPress hosting, domain, premium themes, premium plugins/apps and third-party service fees are **not included** unless specifically stated.
- Third-party services may have their own subscription or transaction fees.
- Major functionality outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately.
- Timelines may change if content, approvals or third-party integrations are delayed.

---

## Revisions

Each project includes **2 revision rounds** within the agreed scope.

A revision means adjustments to the implemented design or content.

Major changes such as:

- Changing the overall design direction
- Adding new functionality
- Adding new pages outside the agreed scope
- Rebuilding completed sections

may require additional time and cost.
